# Privacy Policy for Xtravu

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

tintreach.net ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Xtravu** mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding any information that may be processed when you use our App.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**Xtravu is a fully offline utility application.** 
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect, request, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical characteristics).
* **Usage and Calculation Data:** Any data or metrics you input into the App's calculators, predictors, or converters are processed strictly in real-time on your device. This data is never transmitted to external servers, nor is it stored permanently on your device.
* **App Settings:** The App stores simple user configuration preferences (such as your preferred unit system—Metric or Imperial) locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore. This preference data remains entirely on your device and is used solely to provide a personalized user experience.

### 2. Device Permissions
The App does not require or request any sensitive device permissions, including but not limited to:
* Network communication / Internet access
* Precise or approximate location tracking
* Access to device storage, camera, contacts, or accounts

Because the App lacks Internet permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`), it is technically impossible for the App to transmit any data off your device.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, or cloud persistence services. There are no tracking cookies or background monitoring services embedded within the application.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because our App does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not collect or solicit any data from children under the age of 13 (or any other age group). The App is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

### 5. Security of Data
We care about the security of your information. Since all processing occurs completely offline within your device's isolated runtime sandbox, your data is protected from external interception. However, please remember that the physical and digital security of your device is your responsibility.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App or on our official distribution channels. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
